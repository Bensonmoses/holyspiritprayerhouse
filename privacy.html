Who we are

Holy Spirit Prayer House (IPC), Oduru, West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh, India ("we", "us") runs this website and the YouTube channel @pastorpmoses.

This website

We do not ask visitors to create accounts, and we do not collect personal information through this website. We use Google Analytics to count visits and clicks. It uses cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. See Google's Privacy Policy. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we use your message only to reply to you.

YouTube Manager (our channel tool)

"YouTube Manager" is a private tool used only by our church to manage our own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • update the descriptions, tags and chapters of our own videos;
  • post comments (such as timestamps) on our own videos;
  • organise our own videos into playlists;
  • read our own channel's statistics (YouTube Analytics) to improve our content.

It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect, store or share data about other YouTube users. Access tokens are kept privately on our own computer and are never sold or shared with anyone. By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party access.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We take care not to publish details that could put any child at risk.
